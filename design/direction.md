# Design direction

SisyphusFit's design direction lives in `SPEC.md`, so there is one source:

- R125: the purpose profile (purpose, posture, context, stakes, data, tone 0.25, comparison against the person's own
  history, the requirements that lead and those that do not apply), and the working rule that design questions are
  settled by the dedicated design skills' evaluations.
- R01: the jobs and how often each is done.
- R126: the colour and spacing targets the tokens must meet.
- R50, R52: touch areas and the gates every screen passes.

Read those rules before any design pass. The 6T's app area is 384 × 756 dp (R55).
